5 buying checks

  • Optical input: Confirm the soundbar has a physical digital optical (TOSLINK) input. HDMI-only systems won't meet this requirement.
  • Wireless subwoofer: Wireless typically means audio signal only; the subwoofer still needs an electrical outlet.
  • Rear speaker connection: Check whether each rear plugs into a wall outlet, or whether both must wire into a separate rear receiver module.
  • No AV receiver: Choose a complete soundbar kit with its own amplification and wireless pairing.
  • TV format: Optical can carry stereo PCM and commonly compressed 5.1 formats, but not lossless Dolby TrueHD or Dolby Atmos over TrueHD.

What to ask the seller

“Does this exact model include both rear speakers and the wireless subwoofer? Is the optical input functional? Are all power adapters, remotes, and wireless receiver modules included?”

Used or refurbished?

Check return policies, included accessories, cosmetic condition, and whether the original wireless speakers are paired. Used bundles can be incomplete, so verify the model number and every included component.
